# Sprint 8 Retrospective

## What went well

- Mid-week check-in helped identify blockers early.
- Team members communicated more actively.
- Pipeline dependencies were monitored before synthesis.

## What did not go well

- One member was delayed due to missing data.
- Some tasks still depended on manual confirmation.

## Blockers

The blocker was identified on Wednesday during the mandatory mid-week check-in.

Compared with Sprint 7, the blocker was found about 3 days earlier.

## Process improvement

The biggest improvement was introducing the mandatory mid-week check-in.

This allowed issues to be solved before the weekend instead of delaying the pipeline.

## Action Items

- Continue early check-ins.
- Improve dependency tracking.
- Reduce manual intervention.
