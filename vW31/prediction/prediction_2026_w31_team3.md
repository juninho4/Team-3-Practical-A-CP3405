- QQQ remains below EMA20 and EMA50.
- IWM remains below EMA20.

### Why It Matters

A market led by only a small number of sectors is generally more fragile than one supported by broad participation.

If leadership expands into Technology, Industrials and Small Caps, the current outlook could become more bullish.

Conversely, if the leading sectors weaken while Treasury yields remain elevated, downside risk may increase more quickly than headline index performance suggests.

This factor is not fully reflected by any single agent individually but becomes apparent when comparing all reports together.
